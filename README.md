London Sports Car Sightings
1. Project Overview
   
This project focuses on collecting and documenting data about sports and luxury cars spotted around London. The aim is to build a structured dataset containing information about different vehicles observed in public locations across the city.

The project explores the variety of cars found in London, recording details such as their manufacturers, models, colours, locations and estimated market values.

2. Project Objectives
   
The main objectives of this project are to:

Collect real-world data on sports and luxury cars spotted around London.
Identify different car manufacturers and models.
Record where and when each vehicle was spotted.
Categorise vehicles according to their colour and body type.
Research additional information about each vehicle, including estimated prices and specifications.
Build an organised dataset that can later be analysed to identify patterns and trends.
3. Data Collection Methodology

The data is collected through personal observations of vehicles spotted around London.

Whenever an interesting sports or luxury car is spotted, its details are recorded in the dataset. To help identify vehicles and obtain more accurate information, I use an application called CarDex.

CarDex allows me to scan a vehicle using a photograph. Its AI-powered identification system analyses the image and provides additional information about the car, such as its manufacturer, model, estimated year, horsepower, body style, rarity and estimated market value.

This information helps me identify unfamiliar vehicles and gather additional details that would otherwise be difficult to establish through observation alone.

The information is then organised and transferred into my GitHub dataset.

4. Dataset Structure
   
Each vehicle sighting is recorded as a separate entry in the dataset.

The following information is collected:


Date the vehicle was spotted


Time of the sighting


Vehicle manufacturer


Specific vehicle model


Area or location where the car was spotted


Main exterior colour


Vehicle category, such as Coupe, Convertible, Roadster or SUV


Estimated market value of the vehicle

5. Tools Used

CarDex

CarDex is used as a vehicle identification and information-gathering tool. By scanning photographs of spotted cars, I can obtain additional details and use them to support the accuracy of my dataset.

6. Future Development
   
As the project develops, the dataset will continue to grow through additional sightings around London.

Once a larger amount of data has been collected, I aim to explore different trends and comparisons, including:

The most frequently spotted car manufacturers.
The most common vehicle models.
Which London locations have the highest number of sightings.
The most popular vehicle colours and body types.
Comparisons between the estimated market values of different manufacturers.
These findings could provide a better understanding of the types of sports and luxury cars commonly seen around London.

7. Data Accuracy and Limitations
   
The dataset is based on personal observations, meaning that the amount and variety of data depend on the vehicles encountered.

CarDex provides AI-generated identifications and estimates, which may not always be completely accurate. Therefore, estimated prices and vehicle specifications should be treated as indicative rather than verified figures.

The project will aim to maintain consistency when recording information and identifying vehicles.
